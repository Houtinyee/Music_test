    Paste a tab

    ASCII tabs have no rhythm marks, so timing comes from the spacing. If the beat feels wrong, change what a dash means.

    Techniques it reads: 5h7 hammer-on, 7p5 pull-off, 5/7 7\5 slide, 7b9 bend (7b9r7 bend and release), ~ vibrato, x muted note, (5) ghost note, <12> harmonic, a PM---- line above the tab for palm muting, and for slap bass a line of T (thumb) and P (pop) letters under the tab.
